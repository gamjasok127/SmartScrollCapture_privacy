Privacy Policy for Smart Scroll Capture

Developed and operated by New_Bawoo_Soft
Last updated: August 2026

1. Website Capture Data

Smart Scroll Capture processes webpage content only when a user explicitly starts a capture.

Captured images and related capture data are temporarily stored locally in the user's browser using Chrome extension storage for the purpose of previewing and exporting the capture.

Captured webpage content is not uploaded to or stored on New_Bawoo_Soft's servers.

2. Advertising

Smart Scroll Capture may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including PlayaYield.

To deliver and measure advertisements, limited technical information such as IP address, device type, browser type, advertisement impressions, clicks, and related interaction data may be transmitted to advertising partners.

Captured webpage images and webpage content are not transmitted to advertising partners for advertising purposes.

For information about PlayaYield's data practices, please refer to PlayaYield's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Sale

New_Bawoo_Soft does not sell captured webpage content or personal user data.

4. Purpose of Data Use

Data handled by Smart Scroll Capture is used only to provide webpage capture, preview, export functionality, and related advertising operations disclosed above.
