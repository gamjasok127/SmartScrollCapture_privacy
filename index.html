Privacy Policy for Smart Web Capture 1. Website Capture Data Smart Web Capture processes webpage content only when a user explicitly starts a capture. Captured images and related capture data are temporarily stored locally in the user's browser using Chrome extension storage for the purpose of previewing and exporting the capture. Captured webpage content is not uploaded to or stored on the developer's servers. 2. Advertising Smart Web Capture may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including PlayaYield. To deliver and measure advertisements, limited technical information such as IP address, device type, browser type, advertisement impressions, clicks, and related interaction data may be transmitted to advertising partners. Captured webpage images and webpage content are not transmitted to advertising partners for advertising purposes. For information about PlayaYield's data practices, please refer to PlayaYield's Privacy Policy. 3. Data Sale Smart Web Capture does not sell captured webpage content or personal user data. 4. Purpose of Data Use Data handled by the extension is used only to provide webpage capture, preview, export functionality, and related advertising operations disclosed above. 5.
